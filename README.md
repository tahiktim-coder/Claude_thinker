# Claude Thinker — AI-Disruption Investment Research

## Goal
Identify sectors and companies that will benefit most from rapid AI improvement in the next 1-3 years, **before the market fully prices it in**.

## Investment Parameters
- Budget: ~$3,000 over 5 months (~$600/month)
- Broker: Interactive Brokers
- Style: Targeted sector bets, mix of safer + higher-risk plays

## Focus Sectors
1. **Drug Discovery / Biotech** — AI compressing 10-year drug pipelines
2. **Energy Infrastructure** — Physical backbone powering AI data centers
3. **Robotics / Physical AI** — The next wave after software AI

## Key Principle
> If every AI model gives you the same stock pick, it's not an edge — it's consensus.

We actively challenge "obvious" picks and look for what's under-discussed.

## Methodology
Each sector is analyzed through multiple lenses:
- **Bull case**: Why this sector wins big from AI
- **Bear case**: Why it might not play out
- **Consensus check**: Is this already priced in?
- **Specific companies**: Sorted by conviction level
- **Risk factors**: What could go wrong
