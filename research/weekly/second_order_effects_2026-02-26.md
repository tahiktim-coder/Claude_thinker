## CHAIN 1: AI --> POWER --> GRID BOTTLENECK

### The Causal Chain
AI is big --> Data centers need 10x more power --> Power plants exist BUT the grid can't deliver it --> Grid modernization is THE bottleneck --> Companies that upgrade transmission/distribution infrastructure win.

The market has priced in "AI needs power." It has NOT priced in that the US grid is 40+ years old and physically cannot handle the load. Utilities are rejecting data center connections because transmission capacity doesn't exist. This is a 10-20 year infrastructure rebuild.

### Pick #1: American Superconductor (AMSC) -- STRONG BUY
- **Price:** $34.04 | **Market Cap:** $1.62B | **P/E:** ~11x
- **Revenue:** ~$290M TTM, growing 20-80% YoY depending on quarter
- **What they do:** Superconductor-based power electronics, grid fault current limiters, wind turbine systems. Their products literally increase grid capacity without building new transmission lines.
- **Why the market is sleeping:** Still seen as an "old wind energy company." But their Grid segment is exploding -- 25% YoY backlog growth driven by data center and utility demand.
- **Priced in?** PARTIALLY. Stock is up from $14 lows but still -51% from 52-week highs. Wall Street sees 87% upside. The grid modernization story is just starting.
- **Timeline:** 2026-2030. Every month a new data center is rejected for grid capacity, AMSC's solutions become more critical.
- **10x Potential: YES.** $1.6B market cap in a $100B+ grid modernization TAM. If revenue doubles to $600M at 15% margins, you're looking at $90M profit on a stock that could command 30x P/E = $2.7B, or 67% upside minimum. True 10x requires sustained 30%+ growth for 4-5 years, which is plausible given the structural demand.

### Also Worth Watching (Chain 1):
- **MYR Group (MYRG):** $274/share, $4.26B cap. Electrical contractor with $2.66B backlog in grid/data center work. Revenue projected $3.82B in 2026. Stock already up 300% in 5 years -- may be priced in, but still growing. More a 2-3x than 10x at this point.
- **Itron (ITRI):** $102/share, $4.41B cap. Smart meter and grid intelligence company. $4.5B backlog. Revenue $2.4B. Solid but may be too big for 10x.

## CHAIN 7: CLIMATE TECH --> GEOTHERMAL + FUEL CELLS

### The Causal Chain (Geothermal)
AI data centers need 24/7 clean power --> Solar/wind are intermittent --> Nuclear takes 15+ years to build --> Geothermal provides nuclear-like reliability with oil-drilling timelines --> Companies that can drill deep + extract heat win --> Oil drillers pivoting to geothermal are the sleeper play.

### Pick #4: Nabors Industries (NBR) -- CONTRARIAN BUY
- **Price:** $78.04 | **Market Cap:** $1.14B | **P/E:** 4.73x
- **Revenue:** $3.2B | **EBITDA:** $913M
- **What they do:** World's 2nd largest land drilling rig fleet. BUT quietly building a geothermal ecosystem -- invested in GA Drilling (plasma drilling for superhot rock), GeoX, Sage Geosystems, and Quaise Energy.
- **Why the market is sleeping:** Seen as "just an oil driller." P/E of 4.73 reflects oil-industry skepticism. Nobody is modeling geothermal revenue yet. But Google just signed a geothermal PPA with Ormat for 150MW for Nevada data centers. Microsoft, Meta following. The hyperscalers NEED geothermal and Nabors has the rigs, expertise, and technology partnerships to deliver it.
- **Priced in?** ABSOLUTELY NOT. At P/E 4.73x and $1.14B cap vs $3.2B revenue, the market is pricing Nabors for oil decline. Geothermal is a free call option embedded in the stock.
- **Timeline:** 2027-2030. Geothermal revenue starts showing up on the P&L as hyperscaler contracts materialize.
- **10x Potential: YES.** $1.14B market cap on $3.2B revenue and $913M EBITDA is absurdly cheap even as a pure oil play. If geothermal becomes 10-20% of revenue and the market re-rates from "oil driller" to "energy transition company," the multiple expansion alone could 3-5x the stock. Add revenue growth and you have a credible 10x path.

### The Causal Chain (Fuel Cells for Data Centers)
AI data centers need backup power --> Diesel generators are dirty and face regulatory pressure --> Fuel cells provide clean, reliable backup --> Fuel cells can also provide primary power with carbon capture built in.

- **Priced in?** NO. $355M market cap vs $1.19B backlog. The market doesn't believe in the execution. If even 25% of the 450MW SDCL partnership materializes, revenue could 3-4x.
- **Timeline:** 2026-2028. Data center deployments should start generating meaningful revenue in late 2026.
- **10x Potential: POSSIBLE but high risk.** $355M cap could reach $3.5B if they execute on data center contracts. But negative EBITDA and history of disappointment make this the riskiest pick. Position size accordingly.
