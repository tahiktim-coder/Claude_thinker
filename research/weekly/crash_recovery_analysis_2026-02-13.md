## THE KEY QUESTION: What Separates Winners From Losers?

Morgan Stanley studied 6,500+ stocks from 1985-2024. Their findings:

- **The top 6 wealth-creating companies averaged an 80.3% maximum drawdown**
- **54% of stocks that crash NEVER recover to their prior peak**
- BUT: stocks that drop 80-95% and DO recover deliver **32.6% annual returns** over the next 10 years

### The 4 Things Winners Had That Losers Didn't:

1. **Real revenue from real customers** (not just a vision)
   - Amazon had millions of customers even at $7
   - Netflix had subscribers even during the crash
   - Pets.com had no path to profitability → dead

2. **A platform/network effect that gets stronger over time**
   - Amazon: more sellers → more buyers → more sellers
   - NVIDIA: more developers → more apps → more GPU demand
   - Yahoo: no moat → Google ate them

3. **Enough cash to survive the winter**
   - Amazon had $1B in cash when stock was $7
   - Apple got a $150M investment from Microsoft
   - Theranos ran out of money AND credibility

4. **The underlying market was GROWING, not dying**
   - E-commerce was growing even when Amazon was at $7
   - Streaming was growing even when Netflix was at $8
   - GPUs had no demand beyond gaming in 2002 → had to WAIT for the market
