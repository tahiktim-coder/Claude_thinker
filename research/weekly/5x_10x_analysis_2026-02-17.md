## THE "INDUSTRIAL REVOLUTION" THESIS — WHERE IT APPLIES

You said: *"We are in an industrial revolution. It's not time for 5% returns."*

I agree. But the industrial revolution creates winners AND losers. The AI revolution destroys companies that can't adapt (legacy software, traditional services) while creating asymmetric upside in platforms that become essential infrastructure.

### The stocks with the BEST "industrial revolution" positioning:

1. **FOUR** — Payments infrastructure. Every transaction in the physical+digital economy flows through payments. Growing from 1 to 50+ countries.
2. **PATH** — Agentic AI automation. Every enterprise needs to automate workflows with AI agents. PATH is the operating system for that.
3. **ZETA** — AI marketing cloud. $1.73B revenue guided. If AI transforms how every company reaches customers, Zeta is the platform.
4. **CLPT** — Gene therapy delivery. If CRISPR/gene therapy becomes standard medicine (it will), ClearPoint is the surgical navigation platform.
5. **INOD** — AI training data. Every AI model needs data engineering. But customer concentration is the Achilles heel.

### The stocks that are NOT industrial revolution plays:

- **LUNR** — Moon landing is cool but not an economic revolution yet. Too early, too risky.
- **PL** — Satellite imagery is valuable but already priced for perfection after 468% run.

## KEY INSIGHT: THE BEST 5-10x OPPORTUNITIES HAVE A SPECIFIC PROFILE

Looking at historical 5-10x stocks, the pattern is:

1. **Small enough market cap** that 5x is mathematically achievable (under $5B ideal)
2. **Revenue growing 25%+** with clear path to acceleration
3. **TAM 10x+ larger than current revenue** — room to run
4. **Platform or infrastructure play** — not a single product bet
5. **Approaching profitability** — shows the business model works
6. **Insiders NOT selling aggressively** — alignment with shareholders

The stocks that check ALL boxes: **FOUR, PATH, ZETA, INOD, CLPT**
The stocks that check most boxes: **AMBA, TWST, RLAY**
The stocks that FAIL key boxes: **SOUN** (insiders), **LUNR** (insiders + execution), **PL** (too expensive), **ABSI** (pre-revenue), **RXRX** (pre-revenue + insiders)
