- **Why it matters:** Strategic pivot to being a MATERIALS company (electrolyte supplier) rather than trying to make complete batteries. SP2 line doubling capacity by mid-2026. BMW already testing vehicles with their tech.
- **10x potential:** From $818M to $8.2B requires becoming a dominant electrolyte supplier to the EV industry. If solid-state actually takes off and SLDP is in multiple OEM supply chains, this is plausible by 2028-2030. The $300M cash provides runway.
