## THE IDEA (Why It Sounds Amazing)

Schrödinger uses quantum physics simulations to design drugs on computers instead of in labs. Think of it as:

**Old way:** Try 10,000 molecules in a lab → takes 4 years, costs $1B → 90% fail
**Schrödinger way:** Simulate 10 million molecules on a computer → takes weeks → only test the best ones in the lab

Their platform (Maestro + LiveDesign) is used by **ALL top 20 pharma companies**. Not some. ALL of them. Boeing and Sabic use it too for materials science.

**The money speaks:**
- Novartis paid $150M upfront in Jan 2025 for a research collab
- Eli Lilly expanded their partnership (undisclosed $)
- Takeda acquisition of Nimbus subsidiary → $111M to Schrödinger
- Lilly acquisition of Morphic → $47.6M to Schrödinger
- **Total: $600M+ from co-founded companies since 2020**

100% retention among customers paying >$500K/year. Once pharma plugs Schrödinger into their workflow, switching costs are extremely high.

---

## SO WHY DOES IT KEEP DROPPING?

### Reason 1: The Drug Pipeline Got Smaller (Bad)
- **SGR-2921 was killed** after it was linked to **2 patient deaths**. That left only 2 clinical assets.
- Pipeline went from 5+ programs to just SGR-1505 and SGR-3515.
- They explicitly said they will NOT independently advance new drugs to clinic. They'll partner instead.
- **Translation:** The "we're also a pharma company" story just got much weaker.

### Reason 2: Revenue DECLINED (Bad)
- Total revenue DECREASED 4.21% since last year
- Net income decreased 559.54% since last year
- Drug discovery revenue was guided DOWN to $30-35M from $45.4M prior year
- Software revenue guidance was LOWERED from 10-15% growth to 8-13% growth
- **Translation:** Even the "reliable" software business is decelerating.

### Reason 3: Still Burning Cash (Bad)
- EPS last quarter: -$2.40 TTM
- Profit margin: -68.5%
- They have $401M cash, but burning through it
- Laid off 7% of workforce in May 2025

### Reason 4: Valuation Still Not "Cheap" (Surprising)
- P/S ratio: 3.8x — sounds low but 48% of US healthcare companies trade below 2.2x
- Sub-$1B market cap = micro-cap territory = less liquidity, bigger drops on bad news
- **Translation:** Even after dropping 60%, it's still not screaming cheap vs peers.

### Reason 5: No Near-Term Catalyst (Bad)
- SGR-1505 Phase 1 data: showed 22% response rate. Decent but not amazing.
- SGR-3515 data: not until H1 2026 (could be a catalyst)
- No new drug partnerships announced recently
- Feb 25 earnings = the next real event

## THE HONEST VERDICT

**Why the idea is amazing:** Computational drug design IS the future. Period. The old way (trial-and-error in labs) is dying. Schrödinger has the best platform, all 20 top pharma companies use it, and switching costs are enormous.

**Why the stock keeps dropping:** The BUSINESS execution doesn't match the IDEA.
- Revenue is declining, not growing
- Drug pipeline shrunk after patient deaths
- They're burning cash
- No clear catalyst to reverse the trend

**It's the classic "great technology, bad stock" problem.** The platform is genuinely revolutionary. But a great product doesn't automatically mean a great investment. The market needs to see:
1. Software revenue RE-accelerate (watch Feb 25)
2. A big new partnership (Lilly expansion? NVIDIA collab?)
3. SGR-3515 data in H1 2026 that's exciting

**Your $88 position:** Hold it. At $11.31 your total risk is ~$83. If Feb 25 shows software revenue growth >20% and they announce any new partnership, this stock could pop 20-30% in a day. If they miss, it drops another 10-15% and you lose ~$10 more. The asymmetry on a small position favors holding.
