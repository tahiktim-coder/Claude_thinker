**Previous version had a stupid filter (stocks under $20). This version ranks by what actually matters: growth, valuation, profitability, and moat.**

---

## HOW TO READ THIS

Each company gets scored on 5 things that actually matter:

1. **Revenue Growth** - How fast is the business growing?
2. **Valuation (P/E or P/S)** - How much are you paying for that growth?
3. **Profitability** - Is it making money or burning cash?
4. **Moat** - Can competitors kill it?
5. **Catalyst Timeline** - When does this pay off?

Score: 1-10 per category. Max 50.
