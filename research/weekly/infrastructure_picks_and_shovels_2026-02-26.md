## THESIS

In 1995, the genius didn't buy AOL. The genius bought the companies making fiber optic cable, routers, and server racks -- the stuff WITHOUT WHICH the internet literally could not function.

Today, the equivalent is: humanoid robots need bodies, AI needs cooling, AI needs power, space needs hardware, biotech needs tools, and everything needs quantum-safe security.

This report identifies **22 companies** across 6 categories, all publicly traded, most under $5B market cap, building things the future CANNOT exist without.
