- P/E: -3.23 (negative earnings). This is a clinical-stage story, not a revenue story.

**Profitability:**
- Net loss: ~$103M TTM. Quarterly burn: ~$27-31M/quarter.
- No meaningful margins to discuss. Pure cash burn.
- Cash: $152.5M (as of Sept 2025). Runway into H1 2028.

**Bull Case (3x = ~$7.65 / $1.1B):**

- ABS-201 (anti-PRLR, hair loss) just started Phase 1/2a HEADLINE trial. Androgenetic alopecia = 80M patients in US alone. rNPV of $1.6B per analyst models.
- AMD partnership ($20M investment) + AstraZeneca collaboration validates the platform.
- If either drug hits, market cap could easily 5-10x. $7.78 average PT implies 3x from $2.55.
- H2 2026 interim efficacy data is the big catalyst.

**Bear Case:**
- This is a binary biotech bet. If drugs fail trials, stock goes to near-zero.
- ABS-101 may be inferior to competitors on half-life.
- Revenue essentially zero and declining. $27-30M quarterly burn.
- CEO sold shares in February (26,761 shares at $2.99).
- Morgan Stanley downgraded to Equal Weight in January. Weiss gave a Sell (D-).
- Extremely volatile (beta 2.11). Could easily lose 50%+ on bad data.

**Analyst Consensus:** Moderate Buy (5 Buy, 1 Hold, 1 Sell). Average PT: $7.66-7.98. High: $10, Low: $4.32.
- Needham: Buy, $7 PT. HC Wainwright: Buy, $8 PT. Morgan Stanley: Equal Weight, $4.32 PT.

**Recent Catalysts:**

- Presented at JP Morgan Healthcare Conference (Jan 14).
- CEO insider selling (Feb 2) - small amount but worth noting.
- Next earnings: March 24, 2026.
