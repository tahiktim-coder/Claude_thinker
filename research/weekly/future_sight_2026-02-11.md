We are living through a period where five massive structural shifts are converging simultaneously. Most institutional investors are still allocating capital based on the world of 2020-2024. ## 1. WATER AS THE NEW OIL

### Thesis

By 2030, global freshwater demand is projected to exceed supply by 40%. This is not a prediction -- it is demographics and physics. Meanwhile, AI data centers are consuming water at an accelerating rate (up to 20 million liters per day per large facility). The U.S. alone has 5,400+ data centers, and annual data center water consumption could double or quadruple by 2028. Climate change is compounding the problem with more frequent droughts.

Water is the only commodity where demand is literally inelastic -- you cannot substitute it, you cannot choose not to use it, and the infrastructure to deliver it is crumbling. The EPA's new PFAS limits (4 parts per trillion for PFOA/PFOS) are forcing hundreds of billions in mandatory treatment upgrades.

## 4. ENERGY REVOLUTION BEYOND SOLAR/WIND

### Thesis

The energy transition is real but the execution is harder than the market thinks. Solar and wind are mature. The missing pieces are: (1) grid-scale storage to handle intermittency, (2) 24/7 baseload clean power for data centers, (3) fuel for hard-to-electrify sectors (shipping, aviation, steel). This section focuses on the "second wave" energy technologies that solve these unsolved problems.

### GRID-SCALE BATTERY STORAGE

Following a record 2024 (10 GW installed in the U.S.), battery deployment accelerated further in 2025 with approximately 18 GW installed. Battery pack costs are falling to $105/kWh in 2026. The global grid-scale battery storage market is expected to reach $28.73B by 2029 (30.9% CAGR).

- **What they do:** Design and deploy grid-scale energy storage systems. Joint venture of Siemens and AES. Pure-play on the grid storage megatrend.
- **Why it is non-obvious NOW:** Fluence has been hammered by margin pressure and competition from Chinese suppliers (BYD). But the Inflation Reduction Act's domestic content requirements and geopolitical reshoring trends favor Western suppliers. If tariffs on Chinese battery storage increase (likely under current administration), Fluence is the primary beneficiary.
- **Timeline:** Revenue ramp 2026-2028 as IRA projects deploy
- **Risk Level:** 3/5
- **Conviction:** MEDIUM

#### Tesla (NASDAQ: TSLA) -- Megapack as the Hidden Growth Engine
- **What they do:** Everyone knows Tesla makes cars. Fewer investors appreciate that the Energy division (Megapack, Powerwall) is growing faster than automotive and could become the higher-margin business by 2030.
- **Why it is non-obvious NOW:** Tesla's energy storage deployments have been growing at 100%+ annually. The Megapack factory in Lathrop, CA produces 40 GWh annually, with a second factory under construction. The energy business could generate $30B+ in revenue by 2030 at higher margins than automotive.
