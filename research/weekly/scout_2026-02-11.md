## THEMATIC OBSERVATIONS

1. **The SaaSpocalypse is creating real opportunities.** Software stocks at 20x 2027 earnings -- below market multiple -- is unprecedented. The question is WHICH software companies are actually AI beneficiaries vs. AI victims. Our top picks (FIVN, MNDY, TEAM) are all being sold as AI losers but are actually AI beneficiaries.

2. **Second-order infrastructure plays have already run.** MOD, NVT, CIEN, AGX -- the data center infrastructure buildout beneficiaries have been identified and bid up. The easy money has been made. Look for pullbacks.

3. **Insurance/wealth management AI fear is a template.** Every week a new sector gets hit by AI disruption fears. The pattern is: AI startup launches tool -> sector stocks crater 8-12% in a day -> analysts say "overblown" -> stocks partially recover. This is a repeatable trade pattern for the next 6-12 months.

4. **The "Netflix vs. Cisco" framework works.** The best investments are not in AI builders but in companies that USE AI to destroy costs in boring industries. FIVN (contact centers), IOT (fleet management), EVER (insurance distribution) fit this pattern perfectly.

5. **Cash-rich, beaten-down SaaS is the sweet spot.** MNDY ($1.62B cash), TEAM (strong balance sheet), FIVN (improving FCF) -- these companies can survive any downturn and buy back stock at depressed prices. The ones with cash survive; the ones without don't.
