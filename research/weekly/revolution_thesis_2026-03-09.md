## LESSON 2: WHAT HAPPENS DURING WAR + TECH REVOLUTION SIMULTANEOUSLY?

We've only had this combination a few times in history:

### 1940s: WWII + Early Computing/Nuclear
- The war ACCELERATED technology investment (Manhattan Project, radar, computing)
- Companies supplying the war effort (steel, oil, manufacturing) surged
- **Post-war:** The infrastructure built during wartime created a 20-year economic boom (1945-1965)
- **Returns:** Companies like Boeing went 50x+ from wartime to the jet age

### 1970s: Oil Crisis + Early Computing/Telecom
- Arab oil embargo (1973) + Iran revolution (1979) sent oil from $3 to $40
- S&P 500 went SIDEWAYS for 6 years (1973-1979) — just like now
- Energy stocks CRUSHED the market (Exxon, Schlumberger = 10x+)
- Meanwhile, Intel, Apple, Microsoft were being FOUNDED in garages
- **The lesson:** The war/oil crisis created the BUYING OPPORTUNITY for the next revolution's winners

### 2001-2003: 9/11 + Iraq War + Internet Recovery
- Tech bubble burst, then 9/11, then Iraq war
- Defense stocks surged (Lockheed 10x from 2001-2014)
- But the REAL money was made buying crushed internet infrastructure (Amazon at $6, Google IPO at $85)
- Amazon: $6 → $3,500+ = **583x in 20 years**
- **The lesson:** Wars create fear that pushes GOOD assets to bargain prices
