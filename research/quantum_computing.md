*Analyzed February 2026*

## Why We're Not Investing

### 1. You'd Be Buying AFTER the Move
The 5-10x returns already happened. Buying now means buying the correction of a parabolic move. The easy money was made in 2024-2025.

### 2. Pre-Revenue at Multi-Billion Valuations
- IonQ: $15.5B market cap with almost no revenue
- D-Wave: $8.9B market cap
- Rigetti: $8.7B market cap
- Wall Street doesn't expect practical quantum computing until 2029-2030

### 3. Big Tech Will Crush the Startups
- Google's Willow QPU (Dec 2024) — unlimited R&D budget
- Microsoft's Majorana 1 QPU (Feb 2025) — unlimited R&D budget
- Both have quantum teams 10x the size of pure-play startups
- History lesson: most early-stage tech companies get absorbed or outcompeted by megacaps

### 4. Every Bubble Hallmark Is Present
Per Motley Fool (Dec 2025): "All the historical hallmarks for a quantum computing bubble taking shape and subsequently bursting are firmly in place."
- Insane valuations disconnected from revenue
- Pure narrative-driven trading
- Dilution as only funding mechanism (no profits to reinvest)
- Retail FOMO driving price action

### 5. Dilution Is Guaranteed
All pure-play quantum stocks are years from profitability. They WILL keep issuing shares to fund operations. Your ownership gets diluted every quarter.

## If You Really Want Quantum Exposure

Buy **Alphabet (GOOG)**. You get:
- Willow quantum processor (most advanced in the world)
- Plus a $2T business with 30%+ profit margins
- Plus YouTube, Cloud, Search, Waymo
- Quantum is a free call option on top of a great business

This is what billionaire asset managers are doing — they're buying GOOG for quantum, not IonQ.

## When Quantum Becomes Interesting for Us

Revisit in **2028-2029** when:
- Practical quantum advantage is demonstrated (not just benchmarks)
- Revenue begins appearing at pure-play companies
- Valuations have reset after the current hype deflates
- We can see which startups survived vs which got acquired or went bankrupt

## Bottom Line

Quantum computing is real technology with a real future. But as an investment in 2026, it's:
- Too late (the run happened)
- Too early (practical applications years away)
- Too expensive (pre-revenue at $10B+ valuations)
- Too risky (megacap competition + guaranteed dilution)

**Verdict: Skip. Revisit 2028-2029.**
