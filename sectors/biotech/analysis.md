# Sector 1: AI Drug Discovery / Biotech

## The Thesis
AI is compressing the drug discovery pipeline — target identification, molecular design, toxicity prediction, and clinical trial optimization. What used to take 10+ years and $2.6B per drug could shrink to 3-5 years at a fraction of the cost. Major pharma (Roche, Sanofi, Merck, Bayer) are all signing multi-billion dollar deals with AI biotech firms.

## RXRX — The Consensus Problem

**Recursion Pharmaceuticals (RXRX)** is the most-mentioned AI biotech stock across every source we checked. This is a red flag for edge.

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Market Cap | ~$2.1B |
| Stock Performance | Down 27% YTD, 55% below 52-week high of $12 |
| Analyst Consensus | **Hold** (6 analysts, avg target $7.25) |
| Cash Runway | ~$500M, funded into mid-2027 |
| Key Catalyst | REC-1245 data readout H1 2026 |
| Partners | Roche, Bayer, Merck, Sanofi |
| EPS Estimate 2025 | -$1.57 (still losing money) |

**Verdict on RXRX**: It's the "obvious" pick that every AI model and every financial article recommends. Consensus rating is only "Hold." The stock is beaten down, which could mean opportunity OR value trap. If you asked 3 AI chatbots "what's the best AI biotech stock?" they'd all say RXRX. **That means it's not an edge — it's consensus.**

However: the beaten-down price (-55% from highs) means some of the hype has already deflated. If REC-1245 data in H1 2026 is positive, this could pop. High risk, high reward, but NOT a hidden gem.

## Other Key Companies

### Schrödinger (SDGR)
- Physics-based molecular simulation + ML
- Software is industry standard for molecular design
- Also well-known in AI drug discovery lists
- Analysts expect wider losses in 2025
- **Consensus level: HIGH** — appears on every list alongside RXRX

### Absci (ABSI)
- Generative AI for biologics/antibody design
- Smaller, less covered than RXRX
- Higher risk but potentially more edge
- **Consensus level: MEDIUM** — shows up on some lists but not all

### Moderna (MRNA)
- Using AI for cancer vaccine antigen prediction
- 10 late-stage programs
- Already a large cap — less upside per dollar
- **Consensus level: HIGH** but for mRNA, not AI specifically

### BioXcel Therapeutics (BTAI)
- AI for neuroscience and oncology
- Lead therapy Igalmi, expanded FDA filing expected early 2026
- Smaller and less discussed
- **Consensus level: LOW** — potential edge here

### Illumina (ILMN)
- Not pure AI-biotech, but sells the "picks and shovels" (sequencing hardware)
- Launched BioInsight — AI unit for genomic data + drug discovery
- Benefits from ALL AI biotech companies doing more sequencing
- **Consensus level: LOW for AI narrative** — people think of ILMN as old-school genomics

## The Less Obvious Plays (Where Edge Might Be)

1. **Traditional pharma companies quietly building AI labs** — if a big pharma like AbbVie, Eli Lilly, or AstraZeneca is building internal AI capabilities but the market still values them as "traditional pharma," that's mispricing.
2. **CROs (Contract Research Organizations)** — companies like IQVIA, Charles River Labs — if AI accelerates drug pipelines, CROs process more volume. Picks and shovels.
3. **BTAI or other small-caps** with low analyst coverage where AI thesis isn't priced in.

## ETF Options
- **ARKG** (ARK Genomic Revolution) — ARK-managed, volatile, but broad AI-biotech exposure
- **XBI** (SPDR S&P Biotech) — broader biotech, not AI-specific but catches rising tide
- **IBB** (iShares Biotech) — large-cap biotech, safer but less AI upside

## Risks
- FDA is still slow — AI can find drugs faster but approval takes just as long
- 95% of AI-discovered drug candidates haven't reached Phase III yet
- Cash burn: most AI biotechs are pre-revenue, burning $100M+/year
- Dilution risk: they'll keep issuing shares to fund operations
- Binary outcomes: one failed trial can tank the stock 50%+

## Timeline
- H1 2026: RXRX REC-1245 data readout (key catalyst)
- 2026-2027: First wave of AI-designed drugs entering Phase II/III
- 2028+: Potential first AI-discovered drug FDA approval

## Honest Verdict
This sector is **real but crowded at the top**. RXRX and SDGR are consensus picks with limited edge. The better strategy might be:
1. XBI ETF for broad biotech exposure (~40% of allocation)
2. One high-conviction smaller name like ABSI or BTAI (~30%)
3. A picks-and-shovels play like ILMN or a CRO (~30%)

**Conviction: 7/10** — thesis is strong, but finding non-consensus plays requires work.
